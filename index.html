About the data

Data notes

  • Price rule. Some sale prices in the dataset are impossible, including a sale at $0 and one at more than $80 million. This site leaves out every sale under … and every sale over …. That removes … sales below the minimum and … above the maximum, so the map shows … of the … sales in the dataset.
  • University. Each sale belongs to its nearest campus, measured in a straight line from the home's coordinates. The city name plays no part.
  • Missing values. When a sale has no recorded sale date or number of bathrooms, its popup says "Not recorded".
  • Shared addresses. Some different homes, mostly condo units in State College, share one street address. Every sale is kept and identified by its listing ID, so points at the same spot can be separate homes.
  • Cluster colours. A cluster's colour shows how many sales it groups at the current zoom level, from beige for a few to navy for many. It says nothing about prices.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
